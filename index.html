Guidelines

Competition Rules

Minimum Vote

The minimum voting amount is KSh 50, which equals one vote. All payments are processed securely through M-Pesa.

Maximum Per Transaction

Each transaction can be up to KSh 1,000 (20 votes). You may choose any preset amount or enter a custom amount in multiples of KSh 50.

Unlimited Transactions

There is no limit on the number of transactions you can make. Vote as many times as you like for one or multiple contestants.

Payment Verification

Votes are only credited after successful M-Pesa payment confirmation. No votes are added without a verified transaction.

Competition Duration

The competition runs for 7 days from the official start date. All votes must be cast before the countdown timer reaches zero.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I vote for a contestant?
Navigate to the Vote page, select your preferred contestant, choose a voting amount (or enter a custom amount), then enter your M-Pesa phone number. You'll receive an STK push notification on your phone to complete the payment. Once confirmed, your votes are instantly credited.
What payment methods are accepted?
Currently, we accept payments exclusively through Safaricom M-Pesa. This ensures fast, secure, and widely accessible transactions for all voters across Kenya. Simply enter your Safaricom phone number and approve the STK push notification.
How many times can I vote?
There is no limit on the number of times you can vote. You can make as many transactions as you like, for the same contestant or different contestants. Each successful payment adds votes accordingly.
When does the competition end?
The competition runs for a fixed 7-day period. You can see the exact time remaining on the countdown timer displayed on the homepage and leaderboard. No votes will be accepted after the timer reaches zero.
How are votes counted?
Votes are calculated based on the amount paid: every KSh 50 equals 1 vote. For example, paying KSh 250 gives 5 votes, and KSh 1,000 gives 20 votes. Custom amounts must be in multiples of KSh 50.
What happens after the competition ends?
Once the countdown ends, voting closes and the final results are tallied. The contestant with the most votes is officially crowned the Fashion Crown 2026 Champion and awarded the Fashion Crown Trophy, cash prizes, and an exclusive magazine feature. Winners and final standings are announced on the leaderboard.
What does the "Face of Fashion Crown" title include?
The crowned winner becomes the official Face of Fashion Crown 2026. Privileges include a national media press tour, cash prizes, an exclusive editorial portfolio shoot, luxury brand endorsements, and premier representation as the brand ambassador for Fashion Crown Kenya.
How do I know my payment was successful?
After approving the M-Pesa STK push on your phone, the website will display a success confirmation with your transaction details, including the number of votes credited and the contestant who received them. You will also receive an M-Pesa confirmation SMS from Safaricom.
