Frequently Asked

How do I vote directly from here?
Click "Vote for New Face" on any contender card above. A quick modal will open where you choose your vote package, enter your Safaricom number, and approve the M-Pesa prompt!
When does voting end?
Voting runs until the countdown timer reaches zero. Check the live timer at the top of this dashboard!

You receive an instant on-screen confirmation receipt, and the contender's vote count updates live immediately.
